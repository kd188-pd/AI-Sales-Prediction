About this project.

A compact machine learning application for demonstrating sales demand estimation and business analytics.

AI + BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE

AI-Based Sales Prediction and Business Analytics System

The application trains a Random Forest regression model on a synthetic sales dataset. It combines product category, pricing, discount, marketing spend and calendar features to estimate units sold, then calculates estimated revenue.

PythonFlaskScikit-learnSQLite
✳
◎

Problem statement

Businesses can find it difficult to interpret historical sales and estimate demand consistently. This project demonstrates a simple data-driven workflow for generating a sales estimate from selected business inputs.

✳

Methodology

Generate and clean sales data, create features, train a preprocessing pipeline and Random Forest regressor, evaluate it on a chronological holdout set, then use the saved pipeline for Flask predictions.

⌁

Project outputs

History-only dashboard, demand prediction form, model evaluation metrics, saved prediction history and CSV export. The dashboard does not show the synthetic training records.
